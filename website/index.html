(01) About Who’s behind the work

Hi, I’m Anik — a creative designer from Bandung driven by passion & curiosity. I shape brands, books, campaigns and websites that look sharp and work even harder.

I never stop looking for new styles and new forms to express myself and sharpen my craft. When I design, I always try to be better — and give the best possible result.

Since 2020 I’ve designed for restaurants, telcos, chefs, publishers and start-ups across Indonesia and Singapore — from cookbooks and company profiles to social campaigns, logos and websites. Off the screen you’ll find me behind a camera, shooting photos and editing video.
